Our Work

Programs

Three pillars, built for the people of Wards 4, 7, and 8: youth development, women empowerment, and social justice.

Youth Development

Youth Safe Space

A weekly drop-in space where young people get dinner, structured activities, and safe travel home, every Thursday and Friday from 3 to 9 PM. Held at the ONE DC Black Workers & Wellness Center.

Life Reign also supports the Summer Youth Employment Program (SYEP) through a "Crownz x Reign" partnership, and hosted a Back-to-School Field Day for local families.

Women Empowerment

Women Empowerment

One of Life Reign's three founding pillars, with programming that includes the Women's Wellness Brunch.

Social Justice

Social Justice & Advocacy

Life Reign organizes and shows up: the "Justice for DC's Youth" rally at Anacostia Metro Station, "Operation: Clean Anacostia" (a neighborhood clean-up challenge run with Crowns Inc.), and healthcare-access advocacy in the neighborhood.
