Our Story

About Life Reign

A grassroots community organization built by and for the communities of Wards 4, 7, and 8. Youth development, women empowerment, and social justice, all under one roof.

Meet the Founder

Dominique Moore

Dominique Moore is the Founder & Executive Director of Life Reign and a Washington, DC Public Schools special education teacher of more than 10 years. She started Life Reign to bring that same care to the neighborhood: weekly safe spaces, community organizing, and advocacy for families across Wards 4, 7, and 8.
