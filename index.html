Anacostia · Ward 8 · Washington, DC

Educating the mind without educating the heart is no education at all.

Life Reign is a grassroots community organization in Anacostia. We run on three pillars: youth development, women empowerment, and social justice. Same neighborhood, same families, every week.

Signature Program

Youth Safe Space

Every Thursday and Friday from 3 to 9 PM, Life Reign opens a Youth Safe Space. Kids get dinner, activities, and a safe ride home. It runs out of locations including the ONE DC Black Workers & Wellness Center and Barry Farm Rec Center.
